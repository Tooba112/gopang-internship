About Height Conversion

Height conversion is one of the most commonly needed conversions in everyday life. Whether filling in medical forms, shopping for international clothing, describing yourself on a profile, or interpreting a doctor's measurement, knowing how to convert between centimeters and feet/inches is a valuable skill.

Metric vs Imperial Heights

Most of the world, including Pakistan, measures height in centimeters (cm) or meters (m). The United States and a few other countries still use feet and inches for height. International platforms often require both, so quick conversion tools are frequently needed.

Common Height Conversions

  • 1 foot = 30.48 centimeters
  • 1 inch = 2.54 centimeters
  • 1 meter = 100 centimeters
  • 1 meter = 3.28084 feet
  • 5 feet = 152.4 cm
  • 6 feet = 182.88 cm

Medical Uses

In healthcare, height is used to calculate Body Mass Index (BMI), determine appropriate medication dosages, and assess growth milestones in children. Medical professionals in Pakistan use centimeters, while international medical literature may use feet and inches.
