About Data Storage Conversion

Data storage units measure the capacity of digital storage devices and the size of digital files. As technology advances, storage capacities have grown enormously, making it important to understand the relationship between different units.

Binary vs Decimal Prefixes

Data storage uses binary (powers of 2) prefixes. One kilobyte (KB) = 1,024 bytes (2¹⁰), one megabyte (MB) = 1,024 KB, one gigabyte (GB) = 1,024 MB, and one terabyte (TB) = 1,024 GB. This differs from the SI decimal system where kilo = 1,000.

Common Data Sizes

  • A plain text email ≈ 2–5 KB
  • A high-quality photo ≈ 3–8 MB
  • A 3-minute MP3 song ≈ 3–5 MB
  • A full HD movie ≈ 4–8 GB
  • A modern game ≈ 20–100 GB
  • A home hard drive: 1–4 TB

Internet Speeds and Data

Internet speeds are typically measured in megabits per second (Mbps), while storage is measured in megabytes. Note that 1 byte = 8 bits. To convert download times, divide file size in megabytes by speed in megabytes per second (Mbps ÷ 8).
