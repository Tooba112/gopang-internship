About Speed Conversion

Speed is the rate at which an object covers distance over time. Different industries and regions use different speed units, making conversion essential for international travel, shipping, aviation, and scientific research.

Kilometers Per Hour (km/h)

The kilometer per hour is the most commonly used speed unit worldwide for road travel. In Pakistan, road speed limits are posted in km/h. Most vehicles display speedometers in km/h, and weather reports describe wind speed using this unit.

Miles Per Hour (mph)

Miles per hour is primarily used in the United States and the United Kingdom for road speed limits and vehicle speeds. If you are traveling internationally or consuming American media, you will encounter mph frequently.

Meters Per Second (m/s)

The SI unit of speed, meters per second, is used in scientific contexts and physics. Wind speed in weather research and the speed of sound are often expressed in m/s.

Knots

A knot equals one nautical mile per hour and is used in maritime and aviation navigation worldwide. Air traffic control, ship navigation, and weather services use knots for wind and vessel speed.

Common Speed Conversions

  • 1 km/h = 0.621371 mph
  • 1 mph = 1.60934 km/h
  • 1 m/s = 3.6 km/h
  • 1 knot = 1.852 km/h
  • Speed of light = 299,792,458 m/s
