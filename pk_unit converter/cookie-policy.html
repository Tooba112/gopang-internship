How and why we use cookies on PK Unit Converter

Last Updated: August 9, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how PK Unit Converter ("we," "our," or "us") uses them on our website at pkunitconverter.pk, and what choices you have. By continuing to browse our Website, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide a better user experience, and give website owners useful information about how their site is being used.

Cookies can be "session cookies" (deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (remain on your device until they expire or you delete them). Cookies can also be "first-party" (set by the website you are visiting) or "third-party" (set by a different domain).

2. How We Use Cookies

PK Unit Converter is a lightweight, privacy-conscious tool. We use cookies minimally and responsibly. The types of cookies we may use include:

2.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the Website to function properly. They enable basic features such as page navigation and access to secure areas. The Website cannot function properly without these cookies. They do not store any personally identifiable information.

2.2 Performance and Analytics Cookies

We may use analytics cookies (such as Google Analytics) to collect aggregate information about how visitors use our Website. This helps us understand which pages are most popular, how visitors move around the site, and where we can improve. All data collected is anonymized and does not identify individual users.

Information collected may include:

  • Number of visitors to the site
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Country or region of visitor
  • Browser type and device
  • Referring website

2.3 Functionality Cookies

These cookies remember choices you make — such as your preferred converter tab — to provide a more personalized experience. They are not used to track your activity across other websites.

2.4 Third-Party Cookies

We may allow third-party service providers to place cookies on your device. These providers include analytics services and may be subject to their own privacy and cookie policies, which we encourage you to review.

3. Cookies We Do Not Use

We want to be transparent about what we do not do:

  • We do not use cookies to track your conversion inputs or values
  • We do not use cookies for behavioral advertising or ad targeting
  • We do not sell cookie-collected data to any third party
  • We do not use tracking pixels for marketing purposes

4. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have several options for controlling cookies:

4.1 Browser Settings

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically:

  • View what cookies are stored on your device
  • Delete cookies individually or all at once
  • Block cookies from specific websites
  • Block all third-party cookies
  • Block all cookies (note: this may break some website functionality)

Instructions for managing cookies in popular browsers can be found at:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and Site Permissions

4.2 Opt-Out of Analytics

If we use Google Analytics, you can opt out by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Impact of Disabling Cookies

Disabling cookies may affect your experience on PK Unit Converter. Strictly necessary cookies are required for the Website to function. Disabling analytics cookies will not affect your ability to use our conversion tools but will prevent us from receiving aggregate usage data that helps us improve the site.
