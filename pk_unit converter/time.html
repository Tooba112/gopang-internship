About Time Conversion

Time is the most universally experienced measurement. Converting between time units is useful for project planning, scheduling, calculating durations, and understanding data rates. The second is the SI base unit of time, defined by the International Bureau of Weights and Measures.

Common Time Conversions

  • 1 minute = 60 seconds
  • 1 hour = 60 minutes = 3,600 seconds
  • 1 day = 24 hours = 1,440 minutes = 86,400 seconds
  • 1 week = 7 days = 168 hours = 604,800 seconds

Practical Applications

Time conversion is used in many everyday and professional scenarios. Software developers calculate processing times in milliseconds. Logistics professionals plan delivery routes in hours. Event planners schedule activities in minutes. Scientists work in nanoseconds and microseconds for precise measurements.
