Conversion Accuracy

Our conversions are based on internationally recognized standards and precise conversion factors. For example, length conversions use exact SI-defined values (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly), and temperature formulas use the standard algebraic definitions. Results are displayed with up to 8 significant figures to maintain high precision. For most everyday purposes, our conversions are fully accurate.
When you convert very large or very small numbers — for example, converting terabytes to bytes or millimeters to kilometers — the result can have many digits. In those cases, we display the result in scientific notation (e.g., 1.5 × 10⁹) to keep it readable and accurate. This is standard practice in mathematics and science.
Our conversions are accurate for everyday use, academic study, and general professional work. For highly specialized scientific, engineering, or legal work requiring extreme precision, we recommend cross-referencing with official standards documentation. We display the formula used so you can independently verify every result.

Supported Units

We currently support 9 categories: Length (km, m, cm, mm, mile, yard, foot, inch), Weight/Mass (kg, g, mg, pound, ounce), Temperature (Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin), Area (m², km², ft², yd², acre, hectare), Volume (liter, ml, gallon, m³, ft³), Speed (km/h, m/s, mph, knot), Time (second, minute, hour, day, week), Data Storage (byte, KB, MB, GB, TB), and Height (cm, m, foot, inch).
Yes, we plan to add more categories and units over time based on user feedback. Planned additions include energy (joules, calories), pressure (pascal, bar, psi), fuel consumption (km/L, mpg), and currency conversion (subject to rate updates). If there is a specific unit you need, please contact us using our contact form.
Currently, our area converter supports international units including square meters, square feet, acres and hectares. Traditional Pakistani units like Marla (25.29 m²) and Kanal (505.857 m²) are on our roadmap for a future update. As a workaround, you can convert from square meters knowing that 1 Marla ≈ 25.29 m² and 1 Kanal = 20 Marlas.

Using the Converter

Using PK Unit Converter is simple: (1) Choose your converter category from the navigation or the homepage tabs. (2) Enter the value you want to convert in the input field. (3) Select the "From" unit (what you're converting from). (4) Select the "To" unit (what you want to convert to). (5) Click the "Convert" button. Your result will appear instantly, along with the formula used.
If you enter an invalid value (like text, or leave the field empty), the converter will show a clear error message explaining what went wrong. It will not crash or produce a wrong result silently — you'll always know if there's an issue with your input.
Yes, negative values are supported where they make physical sense. For example, negative temperatures like −10°C are valid. Negative values for length or weight are also accepted by the converter, which can be useful in scientific calculations involving differences or offsets.
The Reset button clears the input value, restores the default unit selections, and removes any displayed result or error message. It's a quick way to start a fresh conversion without manually clearing each field.
