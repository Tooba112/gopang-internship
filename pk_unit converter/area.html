About Area Conversion

Area measurement is critical in real estate, agriculture, city planning, and engineering. Different countries and industries use different area units, making conversion an everyday necessity for many professionals and homeowners.

Area Units in Pakistan

In Pakistan, land is commonly measured in Marla, Kanal, and Acre in real estate transactions. While these traditional units are widely used in everyday property dealings, official government and scientific contexts use the metric system with square meters and hectares. Understanding both traditional and metric units is essential for property transactions in Pakistan.

Common Area Conversions

  • 1 hectare = 10,000 square meters
  • 1 acre = 4,046.86 square meters
  • 1 square kilometer = 100 hectares
  • 1 square foot = 0.0929 square meters
  • 1 acre = 43,560 square feet

Agricultural Use

In agriculture, area conversion is critical for calculating crop yields, planning irrigation, applying fertilizers, and estimating land value. Pakistani farmers often need to convert between local units and metric units when dealing with government programs or international buyers.
